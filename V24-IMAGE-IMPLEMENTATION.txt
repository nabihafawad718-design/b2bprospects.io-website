V24 IMAGE IMPLEMENTATION

Approved 12-image set installed into the six long-form SEO pages.

- Uses one large hero/editorial image.

- Supporting image is constrained to 900px max width.
- Images are responsive on mobile.
- Images have rounded corners, subtle border, and restrained shadow.
- Existing site header, footer, navigation, pricing, contact form, logo, and other pages were not intentionally changed.
