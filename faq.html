FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is B2BProspects.io?

B2BProspects.io is a Chrome extension designed to detect and extract available business information from supported websites, organize it into a preview, and export it to CSV.

Does it work on every website?

No. Results depend on the website's structure, available data and technical accessibility.

Does B2BProspects.io upload my extracted records?

The extension is designed to process extracted records locally in the user's browser rather than uploading them to B2BProspects.io servers.

Can I export the results?

Yes. The core workflow supports exporting collected records as CSV.

Do I need a phone number to contact B2BProspects.io?

No. For now, customer support is handled by email at b2bprospects.io@gmail.com.
