FAQ

Answers before you get started.

Clear answers about the extension and the realtor-data marketplace.

What is B2BProspects.io?

B2BProspects.io is building a Chrome extension for supported website data extraction and a secondary marketplace for ready-to-use realtor datasets.

What does the extension export?

The workflow is designed around reviewing extracted records and exporting supported data to CSV.

Can it extract every website?

No. The extension should only be marketed around supported data sources and workflows. Website structure, access controls, and technical implementation can affect what can be extracted.

What fields are in the realtor datasets?

For the current marketplace schema: Name, Phone, Email, and State.

Will I have to request a quote?

The marketplace is being designed around direct dataset selection and an eventual buy-and-download workflow rather than a manual quote process.

How do I contact B2BProspects?

Use the contact form or email b2bprospects.io@gmail.com.
