FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is B2BProspects.io?

B2BProspects.io is a Chrome extension for extracting publicly available business information from supported websites and exporting results to CSV.

Does it work on every website?

No. Results depend on website structure, available data, and technical accessibility.

Can I export results?

Yes. Extracted records can be exported as CSV.

Does it store extracted data?

The extension is designed to process extracted records locally in the user's browser rather than uploading them to B2BProspects.io servers.
