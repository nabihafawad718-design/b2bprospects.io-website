Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

1. Information processed

When a user starts an extraction, the extension may process website content necessary to provide its core functionality, including publicly available business information from websites selected by the user.

2. How information is used

Information processed by the extension is used to detect available data, extract supported fields, organize records, identify duplicates, show a preview and export results.

3. Local processing

B2BProspects.io is designed to process extracted records locally within the user's browser. Extracted records are not uploaded to or stored on B2BProspects.io servers as part of the core extraction workflow.

4. Data sharing

B2BProspects.io does not sell or transfer extracted records to advertising networks, data brokers or other third parties.

5. Third-party websites

The extension may access content from websites the user chooses to visit. Users are responsible for complying with applicable laws, privacy requirements and website terms.

6. Contact form

If you submit the contact form on our website, the information you provide, such as your name, email address, subject and message, is sent through our form-processing provider so that B2BProspects.io can receive and respond to your inquiry. We do not use the form to sell your information.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, contact b2bprospects.io@gmail.com.
