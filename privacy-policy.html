Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Information processed

When a user starts an extraction, the extension may process website content necessary to provide its data extraction functionality, including publicly available business information from supported websites.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the extension's core functionality, including data extraction, pagination detection, preview, duplicate handling, and CSV export.

Local processing

B2BProspects.io is designed to process extracted records locally within the user's browser. Extracted records are not uploaded to or stored on B2BProspects.io servers.

Data sharing

B2BProspects.io does not sell or transfer extracted records to advertising networks, data brokers, or other third parties.

Third-party websites

The extension may access publicly available content from websites the user chooses to visit. Users are responsible for complying with applicable laws, privacy requirements, and website terms.
